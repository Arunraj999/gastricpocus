What members get

  • A record of learning time. While you are signed in and actively using the block pages, films, evidence pages and tools, the time is added to your record. Nothing is recorded for visitors who are not signed in.
  • Tests with a certificate. Watch a film, answer the questions, and download your certificate when you pass. Every answer is explained with its published source.
  • Certificates for your portfolio. Choose any period and download a certificate of the learning time recorded, with a number that an appraiser or supervisor can check.

Certificates record self-directed learning on this site. They do not state accreditation by an external body.
