PROMs and PREMs for block-only surgery

What patients tell us about recovery and experience when surgery is done under a nerve block without general anaesthesia.

PROM

Patient-reported outcome measure

How the patient rates their own health and recovery: pain, function, sleep, return to normal activity.

PREM

Patient-reported experience measure

How the patient found the care itself: information beforehand, comfort during the block and the operation, and whether they would choose it again.

Tools and standards

Validation study

QoR-15

A 15-item quality of recovery score that takes about 2.4 minutes to complete, with good validity, reliability and responsiveness. It was validated in 127 adults after general anaesthesia and surgery, so its performance after block-only surgery needs local evaluation.1

Delphi consensus

Core outcome set for peripheral regional anaesthesia

Twenty-one international experts agreed ten core outcomes for regional anaesthesia research, intended as a minimum set to make studies comparable.2
