Societies and diplomas

Where the guidelines, diplomas and meetings come from.

United Kingdom

Regional Anaesthesia UK (RA-UK)

The UK specialist society for regional anaesthesia. Courses, guidelines and the annual scientific meeting.

 Europe

European Society of Regional Anaesthesia and Pain Therapy (ESRA)

The European Diploma in Regional Anaesthesia, the PROSPECT recommendations and the World Day of Regional Anaesthesia.

 North America

ASRA Pain Medicine

Practice advisories and guidelines, and the journal Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine.

 UK and Ireland

Association of Anaesthetists

Publisher of the Quick Reference Handbook and the monitoring and consent guidelines.

 United Kingdom

Royal College of Anaesthetists

The curriculum, the National Audit Projects and patient information leaflets.

 Africa

African Society for Regional Anesthesia (AFSRA)

Founded in 2010. Workshops, courses and an annual congress across the continent.
Asia and Oceania

Asian and Oceanic Society of Regional Anaesthesia and Pain Medicine (AOSRA-PM)

Founded in 1989; pain medicine added in 2001. Travelling fellowship and a biennial congress.

Latin America

Latin American Society of Regional Anesthesia (LASRA)

With ESRA, ASRA, AFSRA and AOSRA-PM, one of the five sister societies behind the World Day of Regional Anaesthesia and Pain Medicine.
