Front of neck access

Finding the cricothyroid membrane

StudyWhat it found
Siddiqui 2018, randomised trialAccurate within 5 mm: ultrasound 81%, palpation 8%. Mean error 3.4 mm against 16.6 mm.4
Siddiqui 2015, cadaver randomised trialAirway injury 25% with ultrasound, 74% with palpation. Insertion took longer with ultrasound (196 s against 111 s).5
Kristensen 2016, cross-over trial in obese subjectsTransverse 24.0 s, longitudinal 37.6 s. 90% success with each.3
You-Ten 2015, women in labourPalpation accurate in 39% of obese and 71% of non-obese women.16
Fennessy 2020, childrenPalpation accurate in 29 to 38% of attempts.14
Walsh 2019, childrenUltrasound identified the membrane in all 22 children; height agreed with MRI.13
Oliveira 2017, trainingCompetence after a median of 14 scans; 87% success three months later.17

Tube position

Confirming tracheal intubation

StudyWhat it found
Das 2015, meta-analysis11 studies, 969 intubations. Sensitivity 0.98, specificity 0.98; in emergencies 0.98 and 0.94.6
Gottlieb 2018, meta-analysis17 studies, 1595 patients. Sensitivity 98.7%, specificity 97.1%. Mean time 13.0 s. No difference by operator experience, probe or technique.7
Alsabri 2026, meta-analysis in neonates13 studies, 930 neonates. Sensitivity 93%, specificity 59%.15
Chenkin 2015, learning curveAll 66 physicians proficient at reading clips after two practice attempts; sensitivity 98.3%, specificity 100%.18

Difficult laryngoscopy and tube size

StudyWhat it found
Soni 2025, meta-analysis of 60 studies and 10 580 patientsSkin to vocal cords for difficult laryngoscopy: sensitivity 0.84, specificity 0.81. Skin to epiglottis for difficult intubation: 0.80 and 0.86. Skin to hyoid: area under the curve 0.86. Ultrasound improved identification of the cricothyroid membrane against palpation (odds ratio 3.61) and first-pass success in percutaneous tracheostomy (odds ratio 3.9).21
Carsetti 2022, meta-analysisSkin to epiglottis distance: sensitivity 0.82, specificity 0.79, area under the curve 0.87. No definitive conclusion possible because techniques vary.8
Yao 2017, 2254 patientsTongue thickness above 6.1 cm: sensitivity 0.75, specificity 0.72.9
Ezri 2003 and Komatsu 2007, obesityConflicting results for pretracheal soft tissue thickness.1011
Shibasaki 2010, childrenTube size from subglottic diameter matched the optimal size in 98% (cuffed) and 96% (uncuffed).12
Patel 2021, vocal cordsPooled sensitivity 78% before and 84% after thyroid surgery; specificity 98% and 96%.19
