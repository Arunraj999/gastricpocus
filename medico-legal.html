Medico-legal aspects of regional anaesthesia

What the claims data show, and what consent guidance asks of the anaesthetist.

Claims against the NHS in England, 1995 to 2007

366

Of 841 anaesthesia-related claims concerned regional anaesthesia or analgesia.1

51%

Of regional claims were obstetric (186 of 366).1

82%

Of non-obstetric claims related to neuraxial block (148 of 180).1

£12.7m

Total cost of the closed claims, 34% of the cost of all anaesthesia claims.1

The highest-value claims followed neuraxial blocks and eye blocks, not peripheral nerve blocks. Non-obstetric claims were more likely to involve severe outcomes.1

Consent

Informing the patient

Guideline

Consent for anaesthesia 2017

National guidance built on respect for the patient’s autonomy and the duty to give adequate information. It sets out how the legal frameworks differ across England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland.2
