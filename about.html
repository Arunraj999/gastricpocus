The Northwest Regional Anaesthesia Alliance brings together anaesthetists in the North West of England who teach and practise regional anaesthesia.

Editorial policy

Our evidence rule

Cited

Every clinical statement has a source

A numbered link takes you to the peer-reviewed paper, with its DOI.

Typed

You can see the kind of evidence

References are marked as guideline, meta-analysis, randomised trial, review or descriptive study.

Dated

Each page shows when it was reviewed

Pages are re-read against new literature and the review date is updated.
