Gastric volume calculator

Estimates gastric fluid volume from the antral cross-sectional area measured in the right lateral position. For non-pregnant adults.

Draft for clinical sign-off. Compiled 20 November 2026 by Dr Arun Mohanraj FRCA EDRA, Wrightington, Wigan and Leigh NHS Foundation Trust.

Measure in the right lateral position, between contractions, from outer wall to outer wall.25

How the figure is worked out

  • Area from two diameters. Area = anteroposterior diameter × craniocaudal diameter × π ÷ 4. Free tracing and the two-diameter method are equivalent.7
  • Volume. Volume (mL) = 27.0 + 14.6 × right lateral area (cm²) − 1.28 × age (years). The model was built from 108 patients whose gastric fluid was suctioned under gastroscopic vision.2
  • Threshold. Clear fluid above 1.5 mL/kg, or any solid, is the definition of a full stomach used in the diagnostic accuracy and cohort studies.68
  • Validity. The model applies to volumes from 0 to 500 mL in non-pregnant adults with a BMI below 40. It performed well in a separate study of severely obese subjects.211
  • Pregnancy and children. Use the population-specific studies; this calculator does not apply.1215
