Knobology

Six controls to set before the needle goes in, and four movements of the hand.

1 min 37 s · animated, with on-screen captions · part 2 of the Basics series

Try it

Set up the image yourself

Aim for a target near the middle of the screen, a black artery and white fascia.

Schematic teaching image drawn by the site. It is not a patient scan.

In order

The set-up sequence

01

Probe and preset

A high-frequency linear probe for superficial targets; a low-frequency curved probe for deep ones.1

02

Depth

Put the target near the middle of the screen, with the structures behind it still in view.1

03

Frequency

The highest that still reaches the target.1

04

Gain and time-gain compensation

Fluid should be black and fascia white, with even brightness from the near field to the far field.1

05

Focus

Lateral resolution is best at the focal zone. Where the machine has the control, set it level with the target.1

06

Colour Doppler

Check the target and the planned needle path for vessels before the needle goes in.1

The hand: PART

P

Pressure

Brings the target closer and compresses veins.

A

Alignment

Sliding the probe to place the target on the screen.

R

Rotation

Turning between short-axis and long-axis views.

T

Tilt

Angling the beam to 90° to the nerve.

The PART movements are described in the joint ASRA and ESRA training recommendations.2
