Airway ultrasound in adults

The cricothyroid membrane, tube confirmation and prediction of difficulty.

Before induction

Mark the membrane

Recommended in all patients; ultrasound when palpation is uncertain.2

After intubation

Look for one air column

A second shadowing structure beside the trachea means the oesophagus has been intubated.1

In pre-assessment

Three distances from the skin

To the hyoid, the epiglottis and the vocal cords. Each predicts difficulty with sensitivity and specificity of about 0.8.21

Evidence

What the adult studies found

QuestionFinding
Ultrasound or palpation for the membrane?81% against 8% within 5 mm in subjects with poorly defined landmarks.4
Does it reduce harm?Laryngeal and tracheal injury 25% against 74% in cadavers with difficult necks.5
Transverse or longitudinal?Transverse was faster (24.0 s against 37.6 s); success was 90% with each.3
How accurate for tube position?Sensitivity 98.7%, specificity 97.1% across 1595 patients.7
Can it predict difficulty?Skin to vocal cords: sensitivity 0.84, specificity 0.81 for difficult laryngoscopy. Skin to epiglottis: 0.80 and 0.86 for difficult intubation. Tongue thickness above 6.1 cm: 0.75 and 0.72.219
How long to learn?A median of 14 scans to competence for the membrane, after two hours of teaching.17
