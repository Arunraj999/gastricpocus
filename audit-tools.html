Online tool

Local anaesthetic dose audit

Enter each case on screen, count every injection against the maximum dose, and export the results to a spreadsheet.

Register each audit with your clinical audit department before collecting data. The sheets use an audit number in place of the patient's name. Where no national standard exists, the sheet says so and asks you to set your own.
