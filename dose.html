Suggested dosing recommendations from Table 1 of El-Boghdadly and colleagues.1 The calculator takes the lower of the weight-based figure and the ceiling.

  • Dosing weight. Actual body weight is used up to a BMI of 40, with the ceiling dose applied on top. At a BMI of 40 or more the calculator switches to lean body weight, as advised for obese patients,14 estimated from sex, weight and height using the Janmahasatian model.3
  • These limits are not trial-derived. Maximum doses come from animal work, case reports and pharmacokinetic studies, and absorption varies with the injection site and the patient.2 Treat the result as an upper boundary.
  • Fascial plane blocks. Large volumes placed between muscles are absorbed readily. After a transversus abdominis plane block the plasma concentration peaks at about 30 minutes on average and as late as 90 minutes. Adrenaline lowers the peak, and lower concentrations are advised when it is omitted.1
  • More than one drug. The planner adds together the share of each drug’s maximum. This is a cautious working rule and not a measured value.
  • After the block. Monitor for at least 30 minutes after injection, because presentation can be delayed.1
