Wrong side

Prep, Stop, Block

National guidance that refreshed Stop Before You Block, to be carried out immediately before the needle is inserted.2

Bleeding

Suspected haematoma

The European guideline states that teams caring for patients on antithrombotic drugs must be aware of the risk and competent in detecting and managing a haematoma.3

Anticoagulation guidelines

Nerve

Neurological deficit after a block

The ASRA practice advisory covers prevention, and the assessment of a deficit that outlasts the block.4
