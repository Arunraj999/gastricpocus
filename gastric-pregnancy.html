Gastric ultrasound in pregnancy

The gravid uterus moves the stomach, so the positions and the cut-off values are different. The adult volume formula does not apply.

Position

Semi-recumbent at 45°

Scan semi-recumbent, then semi-recumbent turned to the right. A full right lateral position is often impractical.12

Finding the antrum

Three practical tips

Sit the patient more upright, displace the uterus by hand, and ask for a breath-hold at end-expiration. The antrum lies at a similar or greater depth than in non-pregnant patients.1

Baseline

Similar to non-pregnant

In 59 fasted, non-labouring women at term, gastric volume did not differ from 81 non-pregnant women. The 95th centile was 115 mL, or 1.4 mL/kg.4

Semi-recumbent608 mm²Cut-off recommended by the systematic review for this position.Howle 20205
  • Grade first. The Perlas grade gives a rapid assessment. If fluid is visible, measure the antral area to identify high risk.5
  • Volume models exist but are for research. One model: volume (mL) = −327.1 + 215.2 × log of the antral area (cm²).2 A second, built against MRI, had a 95% agreement band of ±95 mL.3

Physiology

Labour changes the picture

Solids

Emptying is delayed

Gastric emptying of food is delayed by term pregnancy, by labour and in the early postpartum period.5

Fluids

Emptying is unaffected

The passage of fluids through the stomach appears unaffected throughout the peripartum period.5

The systematic review covered 22 studies and 1050 women, and found gastric ultrasound reliable and feasible in pregnancy.5
