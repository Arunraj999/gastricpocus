Using this for a registered audit

Before you collect data

Standard

Set the standard first

A workable standard is that every case stays within the suggested maximum dose for the dosing weight (actual weight, or lean body weight at a BMI of 40 or more), counting all injections together.14

Data

Nothing identifiable

The tool stores no name, date of birth or hospital number, and the cases stay on this device only. Register the audit with your trust before you start.

Limits

What the figures mean

Maximum doses are not trial-derived and absorption varies with the site of injection.2 Where two drugs are used, their shares of the maximum are added together as a cautious working rule.
