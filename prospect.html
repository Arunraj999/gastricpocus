Pain guidance by procedure (PROSPECT)

Procedure-specific postoperative pain management recommendations from a working group of anaesthetists and surgeons, supported by ESRA.

What

One operation at a time

Each review asks which analgesic, anaesthetic and surgical interventions improve pain after a single named procedure, using a systematic review of trials in that procedure.12

Baseline

Basic analgesia comes first

Paracetamol with a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug or COX-2-specific inhibitor is recommended for all procedures unless contraindicated. A block is judged by what it adds to that.2

Method

Updated in 2023

Trials must now be prospectively registered, with the registered primary outcome matching the published one.1

Using it

How to read a PROSPECT recommendation

Example

The same block, two answers

For radical prostatectomy, transversus abdominis plane block is recommended as first choice for laparoscopic or robotic surgery and is not recommended for the open operation.2

Wording

“Not recommended” has a reason attached

Each intervention that is not recommended carries its reason, such as a lack of procedure-specific evidence, inconsistent evidence, or side effects.2
