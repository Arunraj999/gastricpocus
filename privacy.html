Privacy notice

Last updated 27 November 2026.

Visitors who are not signed in

You can read and use everything on this site without an account. NORA² does not record who you are or what you look at. The site keeps your language choice and a few settings in your own browser so that pages work as you left them.

Members

Membership is free and optional. If you join, NORA² keeps:

  • your name, email address, grade or role, hospital or trust, and country, as you enter them;
  • the time you spend actively using learning pages, films and tools while signed in, by page and by day;
  • your test attempts and scores;
  • the certificates issued to you.

These details are used only to run your account, show you your learning record and issue your certificates. They are not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with employers, colleges or anyone else. A person who has a certificate number can check that certificate; they see the name on it, its title, the learning time and the date, and nothing more.

Who is responsible

NORA² (Northwest Regional Anaesthesia Alliance), led by Dr Arun Mohanraj FRCA EDRA, is the data controller. Contact: the email address at the foot of every page.

Legal basis

Your consent, given when you tick the box on the joining form. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

Where it is kept

Member accounts are held by Supabase, a database service, in its London data centre. Passwords are stored by that service in a hashed form; NORA² cannot see them.

How long it is kept

Until you delete your account. Accounts that have not been used for three years are deleted.

Your rights

On the Membership page you can see and correct your details, download a copy of everything held about you, and delete your account and all of it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk.
