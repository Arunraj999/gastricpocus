Airway ultrasound in pregnancy and obesity

Where the neck is hardest to feel, and the cost of failure is highest.

Airway tutorial Video demonstrations Adult Paediatric Pregnancy and obesity Evidence summary Publications Ongoing research References

Draft for clinical sign-off. Compiled 20 November 2026 by Dr Arun Mohanraj FRCA EDRA, Wrightington, Wigan and Leigh NHS Foundation Trust.

Obese women in labour

39% by palpation

The cricothyroid membrane was located accurately by palpation in 39% of obese and 71% of non-obese women in labour. Neck circumference predicted inaccuracy.16

Morbid obesity

90% with ultrasound

After one hour of structured training, 90% of anaesthetists found the membrane with either ultrasound technique in women with a BMI of 39 to 44.3

Poorly defined landmarks

81% against 8%

Ultrasound against palpation, judged against CT.4

Practice

What to do differently

  • Mark the membrane before induction whenever landmarks are not obvious. In these groups that is most patients.216
  • Start with the transverse view. It was quicker in obese subjects.3
  • Do not rely on pretracheal soft tissue thickness to predict difficult laryngoscopy: one study found it predictive, a second did not.1011
  • Gastric ultrasound belongs in the same assessment. See the gastric ultrasound in pregnancy page.
